## One repeatable visual system

- **Master:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 12 seconds, hard cuts at 2 and 5 seconds.
- **Type:** one sans-serif family, Inter if available; semibold white 60 px hook, 44 px dish/restaurant, 48 px estimate, 34 px caveat and series bug.
- **Cards:** charcoal at 90% opacity, 20 px corner radius, 24 px padding; mint accent on the series bug only, never a verification tick or confidence badge.
- **Placement:** keep text inside x=90-900 and y=250-1450, away from the right controls and bottom caption area; put hook/identity above the food and estimate below it, never over the food detail.
- **Readability:** wrap long dish names across two lines, preserve exact names and leave the full identity visible through second 7; inspect the private export at phone size before accepting the cut.
- **Audio:** plate set-down, fork or chopstick sound if owned; optional cleared music stays quiet, and no spoken information is required to understand the order.
- **Footage:** use owned or licensed food footage, hands only; exclude faces and reflections throughout, and do not use a restaurant photo as if it were the filmed serving.
- **Estimate treatment:** from frame 0, show the script's exact three-line estimate stack: "fitsy's AI estimate ~Xg protein" / "~Y kcal" / "estimate for this plate".
- **Series and hook:** the small "[location] orders #N" bug remains throughout; the chosen hook begins at frame 0 over the arriving food close-up, and the dish plus restaurant are also visible from frame 0.
- **Three shots:** 0-2 seconds dish arriving in close-up; 2-5 seconds one food detail; 5-12 seconds the entire plate and any separate components in frame.
- **Reveal:** at 7 seconds expand the same estimate stack into a larger card over negative space beside the full plate; retain the food, identity and caveat, with no app screen or loading animation.
- **End:** at 10-12 seconds replace the hook with the exact save CTA while retaining the estimate stack and series bug; reuse the full-plate shot rather than adding another scene.

The numbered scripts below all use this 12-second timeline.
Their two alternate hooks replace only the opening headline; the estimate stack, caveat, dish/restaurant identity and location bug remain unchanged from frame 0.
This makes the estimate visible in the first second for every variant without requiring viewers to read internal production notes.
No weight-loss, health, accuracy, portion-size or personal protein-goal promises belong in these edits.
