# LA launch strategy: adversarial review

**TL;DR:** Organic Reels is a bounded hypothesis; local reach and usable access are its weakest assumptions.

> **Status:** Independently reviewed; launch readiness unverified · **Evidence checked:** 2026-11-03
> This review supports the [strategy](la-launch-strategy.md); it does not report an executed launch.

## Strongest case against the choice

An established LA creator could put the product in front of a relevant audience sooner than a founder starting from zero.
Paid distribution could also buy a controlled local exposure test.
Those are plausible alternatives, not established outcomes for fitsy.
Founder-made organic content can consume four weeks without attracting enough eligible people to test retention.
Views can reward the restaurant footage while the app adds no reason to return.

The channel choice therefore rests on a judgment about learning with a small cohort, not proof that organic has the lowest acquisition cost.
Organic acquisition economics are also unknown; a future authorized paid test could learn retained-user acquisition costs without already proving them.
The task's prohibition on spending is an execution boundary, not evidence that paid acquisition is inherently ineffective.

### Channel tactics and evidence limits

Use explicit LA/neighborhood text and a tested profile destination; [Instagram's Map announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/08/new-instagram-features-help-you-connect/) describes location-tagged content, not guaranteed local stranger reach.
[TikTok's location-targeting documentation](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/location-targeting) supports the strongest paid alternative, while warning that geography and delivery are imperfect.
[TikTok's June 2025 creative guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices) favors a clear opening proposition, vertical framing and a CTA; applying that paid-TikTok advice to organic Reels is our inference, not proven Instagram performance.

The often-cited “about 50 optimization events in a week” Meta guidance is [secondary reporting](https://terrads.com/blog/meta-learning-phase) here because the [official help page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700) was inaccessible.
An event-volume guideline is not a fixed dollar minimum, and the evidence does not justify saying small paid tests cannot work.
The supplied [Jake Castillo](https://x.com/jakecastilloooo/status/2114741575045586999), [Mau Baron](https://x.com/maubaron/status/2027551137768083619) and [Meta-learning thread](https://x.com/athenaadsops/status/2115605160508498221) references were inaccessible; no benchmark or claimed founder consensus depends on them.
[Apple's attribution documentation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ad-attribution/) describes privacy-preserving campaign measurement, not a ready-made named-user retention ledger for fitsy.

Time two private draft Reels before launch; four hours per week is an estimate including production, checks and follow-up, not a guarantee.
Four Reels each week is the strategist's capacity assumption, not a research-backed productivity result.
The budget allows 2.5 hours for four drafts, 0.5 for publishing/checks and one for cohort follow-up.
If two representative drafts exceed 75 minutes of production, simplify and retest; hold the launch until the cadence fits or explicitly revise the experiment.
The bio destination needs both the approved install route and a separately approved private opt-in form before day one.
If suitable owned visuals are missing, test original dish-name cards and app interactions before committing to the food-first format.
Restaurant photos visible in the app are not assumed licensed for off-platform promotion.
Do not imply the founder ate a meal they have not tried.

## Weak assumptions and failure modes

| Assumption | How it fails | Response within the chosen experiment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The food and fitness audience will value estimates | People want restaurant inspiration but disregard nutrition, or expect exact numbers | Show a real dish and label the estimate; use the concrete meal-choice response to qualify the metric. |
| Organic reaches LA residents | High views arrive from other cities | Name LA and the neighborhood in the opening; confirm residency voluntarily instead of inferring it from followers or geotags. |
| A new user can try the relevant dish | Missing menus, a paywall or an unusable install route interrupts the journey | Complete the app and access checks before day 1; pause acquisition if the same problem blocks the cohort. |
| The founder can make useful content consistently | Four hours proves unrealistic or no suitable owned footage exists | Treat four hours as an estimate, keep editing simple, and resolve footage/availability before starting the clock. |
| Ten positive responses represent love | Courtesy replies, free access or reminders create temporary activity | Require distinct activity days plus a specific example; recheck the same people and distinguish self-report from analytics. |
| The creator list supplies a ready alternative | Food-only accounts, recipe creators or nonlocal audiences pass superficial filters | Require separate LA, restaurant-discovery and fitness evidence; retain unknowns instead of inventing qualification. |

## Evidence that would change the decision

- The founder cannot supply the agreed content cadence, while a genuinely suitable creator can demonstrate LA audience fit and propose usable distribution terms.
- A cohort uses fitsy repeatedly and reports value, but four weeks of local organic experiments cannot reach enough new eligible users.
- A separately authorized paid test demonstrates acquisition of repeat users within a budget Dawit accepts; cheap installs alone would not qualify.
- The product or access path fails the readiness checks; pause acquisition before switching distribution channels.

These are proposed decision criteria, not events observed during this research.
Any creator arrangement, paid experiment, publishing or contact requires a separate authorization.

### Free-access proposal, not an offer

Recommend a time-bounded `pro` pilot covering the full four-week observation, only if Dawit separately authorizes the mechanism.
[RevenueCat documents granted entitlements](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/customer-profile#granted-entitlements) that expire without charging or converting to paid access; they do not cancel a user's existing subscription.
Crucially, even grants used by sandbox testers create production-class promotional transactions, so sandbox-only permission does not authorize issuing one.
No grant was made or verified here.

The supported design is a grant to the verified account ID, refreshed entitlement state, server synchronization, and a real unlocked search/menu/save journey; [Fitsy's subscription source](../../apps/api/lib/subscription.ts) and [webhook handler](../../apps/api/app/api/revenuecat/webhook/route.ts) show the relevant gates, not a demonstrated successful rollout.
Prove expiry or revocation as well as access before promising the pilot.
Do not reuse App Review identities or development bypasses.
If using TestFlight, [Apple sandbox behavior](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/test-and-launch/sandbox/apple-app-store) and the [separate Test Store](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/test-and-launch/sandbox/test-store) are distinct test paths; neither a short sandbox subscription nor a passing Test Store simulation proves four weeks of usable access.
Without an authorized, proven access arrangement, keep the launch clock stopped and make no free-month promise.

### Measurement method

Before day 1, Dawit freezes the cohort eligibility, access period and self-report wording.
Each weekly confirmation records distinct LA calendar dates when the participant used fitsy to explore an available restaurant or dish, their concrete meal-choice example, and whether they would miss fitsy.
Count only participants with usable access throughout both completed weeks, at least two qualifying dates in each, and the positive answer plus example in the latest week.
Keep the account-ID mapping and consented responses private, exclude internal/test accounts, and count missing evidence as unqualified rather than assuming success.
Record the access arrangement so enthusiasm for free access is not mistaken for willingness to pay.
For diagnosis, ask an optional discovery-source question and retain unknown sources; do not attribute every entrant to Instagram or equate a profile click with an install.
This is self-reported attribution, not causal proof or a second success metric.
Do not use the [public feedback board](../../apps/api/app/api/feedback/route.ts) as a private interview form.
Validate any analytics corroboration against known successful actions before interpreting it; no analytics query or production behavior was verified in this task.
