## Overview

Strategy and learnings for acquiring users via UGC (user-generated content) influencer deals. Based on research into the "Locked" app's approach and adapted for Fitsy.

---

## Case Study: Locked App

- **App**: [Locked](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc) — gamified health & fitness app (challenges, streaks, consistency)
- **Revenue per mille (RPM)**: $2-3
- **Target CPM for UGC deals**: $1-1.5 (must be lower than RPM to be profitable)

### Deal Example: @jeremiahjonesfitness

- **Platform**: Instagram (1.5M followers). TikTok/YT handles unknown.
- **Why this creator worked**:
  - Great engagement — real comments, not bots. Good engagement % relative to views.
  - Brand alignment — fitness/health niche matched the app.
- **Deal terms**:
  - Paid $800 with a 600k minimum view clause (MVC).
  - If the first video didn't hit MVC, the creator posts more until hitting it, then gets paid out.

## Outreach Strategy

### Finding Creators

- Find influencers in your niche (macro-friendly food, gym/fitness, weight loss journeys, etc.)
- Reach out with the same message template. Message always starts with **"Paid promo?..."**

### Outreach Template (DM Example)

> Paid promo? Hey, hope you're doing well. I'm the founder of [APP NAME], a [one-line description]. Would you be down to get a deal going?

Key: keep messages looking like they were typed on a phone. No fancy formatting, no professional signatures. High-speed, low-friction vibe.

### CPM vs RPM Economics

- **CPM** = what you pay per 1,000 views
- **RPM** = what you earn per 1,000 impressions (from paid ads / organic, then calculate)
- **Rule**: CPM must be lower than RPM, or the deal is unprofitable
- **Target CPM**: $2-3
- **Deal structure**: Always use a Minimum View Clause (MVC) — creator doesn't get paid until views hit the floor
- Open question: how to calculate RPM? Start with paid ads / organic acquisition cost, then derive.

### Creator Vetting Criteria

1. **Prioritize conversions, not views** — go for creators with good content, not just followers.
2. **Check engagement quality** — real comments from real people, not bots. Look at engagement % relative to views.
3. **Brand alignment** — creator's content and audience must overlap with your app's target user.
4. **No agency representation** — if they have an agency email in their bio, it's too late. Their management will eliminate any alpha in that deal. Filter out bios containing "mgmt," "agency," or "enquiries."
5. **The creative must frame the product as a solution to a problem that resonates, not a toy.**

### Negotiation Process

- Meet with the creator 1-on-1 in a Zoom call to go over the agreement and negotiate.
- Use the Minimum View Clause (MVC) structure: creator doesn't get paid until views hit the floor.
- Target CPM: $2-3.

### The Two-Message Playbook

The VA outreach is intentionally simple — two messages only:

1. **Opener**: "Paid promo? Hey, hope you're doing well. I'm the founder of Fitsy, a macro-aware restaurant finder. Would you be down to get a deal going?"
2. **If interested**: "Awesome, give me a call at (XXX)..." or "Ok let me know when you're free"

Then you (the founder) take the call directly.

## Key Takeaways

- UGC deals at sub-RPM CPMs are the highest-leverage paid acquisition channel for a fitness app.
- The "Paid promo?" opener is a pattern interrupt — casual, direct, founder-to-creator.
- MVC clauses de-risk the spend: you only pay for proven reach.
- Unmanaged creators (no agency) are where the alpha is — lower rates, more authentic content, flexible terms.
