# LA launch strategy

**TL;DR:** Use founder-made organic Instagram Reels to find 10 LA users who love fitsy and return weekly.

> **Status:** Proposed · **Evidence checked:** 2026-11-03 · **Owner:** Dawit
> This is a launch recommendation, not permission to publish, contact people, grant access or spend.
> The four-week clock starts after Dawit approves execution and the readiness checks below pass.

## Positioning and hook

For LA restaurant explorers who loosely care about nutrition, fitsy answers: “Does this dish fit my goals?”
Show appealing food, then a useful estimate, at supported restaurants including independent spots.
The hook: **“New LA spots that fit your goals.”**
Use one dish, one action and visibly labeled estimates; avoid precision promises, diet guilt and strict tracking language.

“Competitors only cover chains” is not a supportable claim: [MenuFit advertises local favorites](https://menufitapp.com/), while [LeanBites foregrounds chains](https://leanbites.app/) without proving chain-only coverage.
Demonstrate local usefulness; superior breadth or accuracy remains unproven.

## Choose one channel

**Organic content, on Instagram Reels first.**
My judgment: control over local examples and feedback makes this the best first experiment.
No existing founder audience is assumed.
Start faceless with owned food footage, on-screen menu and app captures, and text or voiceover.
Use "surprisingly healthy slept-on LA spots/dishes" as an internal content angle about specific dishes, not a claim about any venue.
The [LA dish shortlist](la-dish-shortlist.md) is a research queue; publish nutrition-led copy only after checking the exact current menu dish and Fitsy estimate and confidence.
Phrase every number as an estimate, for example "fitsy estimates ~45g protein," only when that value is verified in Fitsy.
[Instagram supports testing Reels with non-followers](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/inspiring-creativity-that-brings-people-together/), without guaranteeing LA reach.
Try Trial Reels if available; otherwise use ordinary Reels.

| Option | Decision for these four weeks |
| --- | --- |
| Organic content | Choose: demonstrate real dishes and learn which promise brings people back. |
| Paid ads | Defer: [local targeting](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/location-targeting) is useful, but retained-user acquisition economics are unknown. |
| UGC / creators | Defer: purchased footage still needs distribution; creator-posted content needs a verified local audience and agreed terms. |

[MenuFit already recruits creator videos for Meta ads](https://creators.menufitapp.com/), including submissions that creators never post themselves.
The [creator research](la-creators.csv) is a reserve, not a second launch channel.

## Four-week experiment

All quantities below are planning judgments, not conversion forecasts.
Estimate **four founder hours weekly**: 2.5 for production, 0.5 for publishing/checks and one for cohort follow-up.
Time two private drafts; if production exceeds 75 minutes, simplify and retest before starting.
From day one, use the CTA **“Find your next LA meal in fitsy - link in bio.”**
That destination pairs the approved install route with an optional private cohort form requesting consent for weekly follow-up.

| When | Dawit's action after execution approval |
| --- | --- |
| Before day 1 | Compare Silver Lake and Hollywood, which are audit candidates rather than proven coverage leaders. Require five independent venues and ten usable main dishes across three cuisines in the actual app. Confirm installation, checkout terms, four-week access and private measurement. |
| Week 1 | Publish four original 15-30 second Reels using owned footage and actual app screens, with voiceover or text. Name LA and the neighborhood immediately. Test two formats twice: “A new spot after your workout” and “Two dishes for your next dinner out.” Enroll opted-in LA participants from this week. |
| Week 2 | Publish four more Reels using the stronger format. Show one dish and its labeled estimate. Verify the CTA path; enroll only opted-in, eligible LA users. |
| Week 3 | Publish four Reels; apply the rule below after two complete measurement weeks. Diagnose access, coverage and local distribution separately. |
| Week 4 | Publish four Reels unless acquisition is paused; refine one hook or CTA. Recheck the original cohort and include newly matured week-two entrants. Continue only if the goal holds; otherwise end and document the limitation. |

Use owned or licensed footage; no new restaurant purchases are assumed.
The [review and product evidence](la-launch-strategy-review.md) define the readiness checks and their current limits.

## One metric

**Weekly loved repeat users: target 10.**
Count a target-audience LA resident once if they had usable access and explored a restaurant or dish on at least two distinct days in each of the last two completed Monday-Sunday weeks (`America/Los_Angeles`), and would miss fitsy, naming a meal choice it helped in the latest week.
Exclude staff, test accounts and unknowns; an app open, install, like or view alone does not qualify.
Freeze the private weekly confirmation before day 1, record dates and responses with consent, and label the count self-reported.
Week-one entrants mature after weeks 2-3; week-two entrants after weeks 3-4.
Later entrants cannot qualify within this experiment, so last-week recruitment cannot resolve retention.
This remains an early retention signal.

**End-of-week-3 decision, reviewed Monday:** 10 or more means continue; 3-9 means refine the best format for one more week.
Start on Monday so the windows align.
At 0-2, if ten eligible participants had usable access throughout both weeks, pause acquisition and investigate utility or access before more content.
Otherwise, demand is inconclusive: allow one week to improve the local hook and install path, then reassess.
These thresholds are management rules, not statistically validated benchmarks.
No automatic week-3 move to creators or ads.

## Skip now

Paid campaigns, creator outreach or deals, in-person and trainer outreach, TikTok cross-posting, SEO projects, automated content production, new onboarding code, nationwide promises and the superseded 500 MAU target.
